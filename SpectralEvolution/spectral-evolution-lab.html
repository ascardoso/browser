What makes this flute-like?

A real flute starts with an air jet striking an edge. That jet feeds resonances in the tube. This educational model approximates the process with a breath-driven harmonic source, air-column resonances, and filtered turbulent noise.

sound ≈ resonant tone + breath noise

What are we measuring?

The waveform, spectrum and spectrogram are read from the synthesizer's actual audio output. The horizontal tracks are the fundamental and harmonics; their changing brightness shows the sound evolving.

fₙ ≈ n × f₀   (air-column modes)

This is a physical-model-inspired instrument, not a full fluid-dynamics simulation or a sampled flute. The simplified synthesis stages are exposed so you can hear and observe their effects. Start at a comfortable volume.

MIDI chord keyboard

Press “Connect MIDI” and allow access. Or click the keys or use your computer keyboard.
Play some notes

MIDI input needs Chrome, Edge or Opera (Safari doesn’t support Web MIDI). If the browser blocks access inside this preview, open the page’s link in its own tab. Computer keys: A–K are the white keys from C4, W E T Y U the black keys; Z / X shift the octave. The keyboard shows C2–C7; notes outside that range still sound and count toward the chord.
